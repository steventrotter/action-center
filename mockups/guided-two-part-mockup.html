Comment by October 6 to keep the Roadless Rule

Why This Matters

The U.S. Department of Agriculture has filed a proposed rule to rescind the 2001 Roadless Area Conservation Rule in its entirety. Conservation groups call this the last scheduled chance for the public to weigh in before it is finalized.

The rule protects about 45 million acres of national forest, nearly 2 million of them in Oregon, including more than 13,000 acres around Mount Hebo in the Siuslaw that nothing else protects. The comment period was extended to Tuesday, October 6. A comment that names a place and says what you do there is far harder to set aside than a form letter.
